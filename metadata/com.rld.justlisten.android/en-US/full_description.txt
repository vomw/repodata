<p><i>Just Listen</i> is a simple music media player that is dependent on <a href='https://audius.org/' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>Audius</a> APIs.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Search songs &amp; playlists on the Audius infrastructure</li><li>Listen to songs &amp; audiobooks in the background for free</li><li>Create your private playlists</li><li>Adds songs to favorite</li><li>No data leaves your phone, other than the calls to get information from Audius APIs</li></ul>
