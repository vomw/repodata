<p><i>Osm Go!</i> is a mobile application that allows you to enrich OpenStreetMap directly in the field. Mapping your direct environment has never been so easy and fast. Keep your eyes open and have the right reflex!</p><p><i>Osm Go!</i> has been designed to map the POIs (equipment, shops, etc.) that are around you during your Sunday walks – so you don’t have to be an expert to use it!</p>
