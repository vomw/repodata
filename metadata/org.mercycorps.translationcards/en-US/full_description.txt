<p>Translation Cards allows you to create and use simple audio flashcards: tap a card and hear it spoken aloud in a different language. This app is for field workers, such as NGOs and volunteers, who need to communicate in foreign languages.</p><ul><li>Tap a card to hear a common phrase</li><li>Create your own cards in three steps</li><li>Easy to learn, easy to use in the field</li><li>Currently supports Farsi, Pashto, and Arabic</li></ul>
