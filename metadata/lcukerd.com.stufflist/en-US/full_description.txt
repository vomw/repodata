<p><i>Stuff List</i> helps you keep track of items for a trip or an event. You can create a list of items required and make sure you don’t forget to take it. It also makes sure that you don’t forget to bring back any item from your journey.</p><p><b>Note:</b> this app's repo seems no longer maintained, so there won't be further updates. Should the app no longer work, please let us know.</p>
