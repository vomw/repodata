<p><i>Portarius</i> is an application that uses Portainer API to manage your docker containers.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>User management</li><li>See running/stopped containers (and also start/stop and restart them)</li><li>See container logs, mounts, and status</li><li>Biometric authentication</li><li>Data is stored with AES256 encryption</li><li>Light/Dark theme</li></ul><p><b>Usage:</b></p><ul><li>Have a Portainer service running</li><li>Connect to your service (ex. https://portainer.example.com OR http://192.168.x.x:9000)</li></ul>
