<p><i>Switch My Slot</i> is an Android app that helps you switch between android A & B slots easily without having to enter commands in adb or fastboot shell. You don't even need TWRP recovery to use it.</p><p><br><b>Requirements:</b></p><ul><li>Device with A/B partitions</li><li>Device platform should be ARM64</li><li>Root access on android device</li><li>If you are using MagiskSU flash <a href='https://github.com/roihershberg/bootctl-binary' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>this module</a> to mount the bootctl utility.</li></ul>
