<p><i>PDF to Images</i> is a simple Android app for converting PDF files to images, for example to import into an expenses app which doesn't support PDF files.</p><p>This app was born out of frustration with existing offerings having paywalls, watermarks, ads and tracking – this has none of that. It was written to service my own limited need and is not well tested for full pdf support or support of larger files.</p><p>The app will only load the first 10 pages of a PDF.</p>
