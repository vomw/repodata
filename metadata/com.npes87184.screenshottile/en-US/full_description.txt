<p>Share screenshot in better and smooth way.</p><p>Screen Share Tile works through its quick settings tile, before using it, tile needs to be added on notification panel manually. After that, the process is easy:</p><ol><li>Click quick settings tile</li><li>Crop screenshot</li><li>Share it!</li></ol><p>To add the tile, please follow these steps:</p><ol><li>Pull down completely the notification shade.</li><li>Tap the edit button (on stock Android it is a pencil). The quick settings editing layout will expand and display all the available tiles.</li><li>Scroll down and find the screen share tile.</li><li>Drag the tile up to the desired position.</li><li>That's it!</li></ol><p><b>Permissions:</b></p><ul><li>Screen Recording - required in order to capture screenshots</li></ul>
