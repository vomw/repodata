<p><i>Macro Deck</i> is an open source software to use your smartphone, tablet or almost any touch screen device with a internet browser as a simple macro pad or even as an powerful automation solution for streaming, gaming, content creation and more.</p><p><b>!!! This version is only for Macro Deck 2 !!!<br>!!! This is just the companion app, you also need the <a href='https://github.com/Macro-Deck-org/Macro-Deck' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>Macro Deck application</a> on your PC!!!</b></p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Open source</li><li>Plugins</li><li>Icon packs</li><li>Web client</li><li>Built in package manager (to download plugins and icon packs)</li><li>Logic and global variables</li><li>Multiple profiles</li><li>Unlimited folders</li><li>Discord community</li></ul>
