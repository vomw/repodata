<p>TWRP Data backups do not include Internal Storage, which is the emulated SD card that stores your photos, videos, downloads, and more. This means that in the event you transfer phones or completely wipe data, all of that is gone! Users must resort to error-prone manual backups instead.</p><p><i>Tipatch</i> is here to help. Simply run it on a TWRP image file to produce a new one which backs up all internal storage, making backups complete.</p><p>If you are rooted, it’s even better! Simply tap a button to patch your current recovery in-place in seconds.</p>
<p><b>NOTE:</b> <i>Tipatch</i> is <a href='https://github.com/kdrag0n/tipatch/issues/3#issuecomment-1123099564' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>no longer maintained</a>.</p>
