<p>This is a Recording Webcam - Camera App for android TV and Android Phone to capture or record video and upload the file to a server.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li><b>Capture:</b> Take photos with an existing camera, change the camera used, turn the flash on and off, and change photo quality</li><li><b>Recording:</b> Record video with an existing camera, change the camera used, turn the flash on and off, and change the quality of the recorded video</li><li><b>Upload:</b> Send photos or videos to the server via url</li></ul>
