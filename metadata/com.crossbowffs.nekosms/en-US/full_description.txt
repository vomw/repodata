<p><i>NekoSMS</i> is a pattern-based text message blocker for Android. It requires a rooted phone running Android 4.4 KitKat or newer, and the Xposed framework installed.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Works with all SMS apps, stock or third party</li><li>Block messages based on sender and/or content</li><li>Supports regular expressions, wildcard patterns, and more</li><li>Backup and restore your filter rules across devices</li><li>Free, both as in beer and in speech</li><li>No internet access, no ads, no telemetry</li></ul>
