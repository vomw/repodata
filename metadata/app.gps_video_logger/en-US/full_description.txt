<p><i>GPS Video Logger</i> is an ad-free open source lightweight app to record video and geographical position simultaneously. GPS logging and video tracking is done in an optimized manner.</p><p>The app offers two modes of recording: Video Mode and Timelapse Mode. It also provides a video player to view your video and location on map side by side. (Video and GPS player)</p><p>Recorded journeys are stored directly to your phone and can be accessed at <code>Android/data/app.gps_video_logger</code>.</p>
