<p><i>Fixed Deposit Tracker</i> is a comprehensive app designed to help you manage your fixed deposits efficiently. With features that allow you to add, view, update, and delete fixed deposits, it ensures you stay informed about your investments.</p><p>This app operates completely offline, ensuring your data is always accessible without requiring an internet connection.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>➕ Add, View, Update & Delete Fixed Deposits</li><li><b>🔔 Notifications:</b> Receive notifications 3 days before maturity and on the maturity date</li><li><b>🧮 Fixed Deposit Calculator:</b> Interactive text fields and sliders for easy calculations</li><li><b>📂 Export Data:</b> Option to export your fixed deposit data into a CSV file</li><li><b>📊 Glance Widget:</b> Quickly view total invested and maturity amounts</li><li><b>🌗 Dark/Light Mode:</b> Toggle between dark and light themes</li><li><b>🎨 Dynamic Color:</b> Enable or disable dynamic color settings</li><li><b>🔗 App Shortcuts:</b> Quick access to the add FD screen and calculator screen</li></ul>
