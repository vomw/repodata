<p>Tired of pressing the power button/swiping the screen every time an alarm rings? No worries! With this app, you can simply shake your phone and dismiss the alarm.</p><p><br><b>Salient features:</b></p><ol><li>Works offline.</li><li>No advertisements.</li><li>Completely open-source app.</li><li>Alarms are unique by alarm time. This means that you cannot have two alarms at the same time, even if they are on different dates.</li><li>Each alarm is independent of other alarms. This means that the alarm volume, ringtone, etc. will not be carried over to another alarm unless you do so manually.</li><li>Inbuilt dark theme, even in phones that do not support it.</li><li>Snooze your alarm as many times as you want with custom snooze options.</li><li>When updates are released, you will be notified within the app itself.</li><li>The alarm is handled by a service which has almost no dependency on the UI. So, even if your UI freezes, the alarm will ring and can be dismissed.</li><li>Uses latest Android Room database for storing alarms.</li><li>Actively maintained app. Bug reports will be worked upon with high priority.</li></ol>
