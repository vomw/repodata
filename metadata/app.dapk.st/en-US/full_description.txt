<p><i>SmallTalk</i> is a minimal messenger using the Matrix protocol. It has a comparingly tiny app size and focuses on reliability and stability. Its feature set is rather bare-bones:</p><ul><li>Login with Matrix ID/Password</li><li>Combined Room and DM interface</li><li>End to end encryption</li><li>Message bubbles, supporting text, replies and edits</li><li>Push notifications (DMs always notify, Rooms notify once)</li><li>Importing of E2E room keys from Element clients</li><li><a href="https://unifiedpush.org/" target="_blank" rel="noindex nofollow noopener">UnifiedPush</a></li></ul><p>More to come in the future.</p>
