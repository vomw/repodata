<p>This app allows to quickly toggle any of the system profiles of LineageOS using a widget from the home screen. It only works if you have LineageOS 14 or higher installed in your device. Tested up to LineageOS 18.0.</p><p>The functionalities are:</p><ul><li>Having the widget in the home screen you know easily which profile is active at any moment.</li><li>Customize the icons and colors of the profiles to be shown in the widget.</li><li>Get a notification when some special profile is activated. For example, if you have a profile that disables the lock screen and it is automatically activated in some circumstances, you may want to get a notification when it is activated.</li><li>Two ways of switching from one profile to another:<ul><li>Using a popup window that shows the list of existing profiles and lets you select the one to activate.</li><li>Quick toggle from one profile to the next every time you click in the widget.</li></ul></li><li>Put a tile in the quick settings bar for the system profiles. Although this functionality comes already built-in in Lineage OS, some releases seem to lack it.</li></ul><p>This app is open source, with no ads and no in-app purchases, but provided with no warranty.
