<p><i>Kill Domain Verification</i> is an Xposed module which prevents the system (and also yourself) from allowing apps to always open supported links. It also enables you to open supported links in apps which are not "verified" on Android 12 and above.</p><ul><li><b>Module function:</b> always ask when opening http / https links, instead of opening directly in the app</li><li><b>Side effect:</b> Can't open the link directly in the app even if you set it manually; Android 12+ will show the unverified app when you select it</li></ul><p>Theoretical support for Android 6 and later.</p>
