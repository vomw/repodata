<p><i>k9t9</i> is a minimalist, Nokia-inspired T9 keyboard for Android.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Predictive text entry for English language</li><li>Barebones punctuation (through the 1 key)</li><li>4 entry modes (cycled using pound key)<ul><li>Lowercase word</li><li>Capitalized word</li><li>All caps word</li><li>Numbers</li></ul></li><li>Recomposing previously-entered words</li></ul>
