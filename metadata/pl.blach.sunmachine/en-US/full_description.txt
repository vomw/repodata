<p><i>Sunmachine</i> is an application to control smart lighting system. It features:</p><ul><li>Manual and auto operating modes.</li><li>In auto mode, it uses motion and light sensors to turn the light on or off automatically.</li><li>Supports WS2812, SK6812 or similar LED strips.</li><li>Supports both RGB and WWA LEDs.</li><li>Built-in scheduler that allows to change mode, brightness, hue, saturation or temperature at a specific time.</li></ul>
