<p><i>Mock my GPS</i> is a light-weight Android app that mocks the GPS and Network location providers of your device. It allows you to fake your location, so apps believe you‘re somewhere else – which can be a „fixed location“ or a „trip simulation“ (movement between two locations in a given time frame).</p><p>Don’t let the „old-fashioned design“ scare you away; no „fancy frameworks“ also means no extra weight, take a look at the size of the app :)</p><p><i>Mock my GPS (UnifiedNlp Backend)</i> is an optional addition to <a href='https://apt.izzysoft.de/packages/com.github.warren_bank.mock_location'>Mock my GPS</a> providing mocked location data to UnifiedNlp while <i>Mock my GPS</i> is running.</p>
