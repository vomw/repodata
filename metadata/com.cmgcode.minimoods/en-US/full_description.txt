<p><i>Mini Moods</i> is an ad-free, open-source, no-frills mood tracker that gives you full control over your data.</p><p><br><b>Features</b></p><ul><li><strong>Minimal interface</strong>. Mini Moods provides a simple, clear and elegant user interface that makes entering and tracking your data super simple.</li><li><strong>No Frills</strong>. Other apps often include accounts, mandatory internet connections, ads, event logging or daily descriptions whereas Mini Moods was created to simplify the process of mood tracking.</li><li><strong>Monthly Tracking</strong>. All your historical moods are visible on a monthly basis within the app. You can select a single month at a time to view daily moods and trends.</li><li><strong>Data Export</strong>. To view more than one month at a time, Mini Moods allows you to export your data in CSV format.</li><li><strong>Widget</strong>. The Mini Mood widget allows you to view, modify or add today's mood from your desktop without the need to open an app.</li><li><strong>Dark Mode</strong>. Native support for dark-mode built into all aspects of the app including widgets.</li><li><strong>Open Source & Completely Ad-Free</strong>. In addition to giving you access to all your data through the CSV export, Mini Moods is open source and contains no ads. You can view the source and contribute <a href='https://github.com/CampbellMG/MiniMoods' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>here</a>.</li></ul><p><i>Mini Moods</i> uses the <code>INTERNET</code> permission for crash reporting via Sentry.</p>
