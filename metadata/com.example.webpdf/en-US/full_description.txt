<p><i>MHTML-Viewer</i> is an Android App without ads that lets you view and save mhtml files. Uses WebView's saveWebArchive method.</p><p><br><b>Permissions:</b></p><ul><li>Internet: Let's the webView access the internet.</li><li>Read/Write Files: Needed to save and read mhtml files. Can't save to or read from SD cards or anything like that.</li></ul><p><b>Privacy</b></p><p>This app doesn't collect any data.</p>
