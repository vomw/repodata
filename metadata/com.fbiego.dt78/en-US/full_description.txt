<p><i>DT78</i> is an alternative app for the DT78 Smartwatch. Also works with DT92 &amp; DT66. May work with other watches that use the WearFit 2.0 app.</p><p>The camera may not work on some devices and on others only the back camera may work. Rooted users can long press to switch between In-app or External camera.</p><p><b>Note:</b> This app has been officially deprecated and a successor was named, so you won't see any updates here anymore: the app's repo also was archived meanwhile. Unfortunately, the successor seems not to be F/LOSS, and comes with proprietary components, Ads, and Tracking.</p>
