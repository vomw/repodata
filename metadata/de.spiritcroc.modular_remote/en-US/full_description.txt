<p>The aim of this app is to provide the user a possibility to fully create their own remote out of several modules.</p><p>Currently supported remotely control-able devices:</p><ul><li>Basically any device controllable via TCP, you just have to look up the commands when you set up your remote</li><li>Advanced support for pioneer receivers (via network): Select commands &amp; responses (not all commands &amp; responses supported yet)</li><li>Any device that supplies a browser-based user-interface</li><li>Any device control-able with widgets supplied by an external app</li></ul><p>Available modules:</p><ul><li>Button</li><li>Toggle button</li><li>Dropdown list</li><li>Display</li><li>Command line</li><li>WebView</li><li>Widget</li><li>Scroll containers</li></ul><p>You can also configure multiple pages to best suit your needs depending on different occasions. If your launcher supports creating shortcuts (like e.g. NovaLauncher), you can create home screen shortcuts to your pages.</p>
