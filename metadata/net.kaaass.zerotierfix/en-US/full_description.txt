<p><i>Zerotier Fix</i> is an unofficial <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZeroTier' target='_blank'>Zerotier</a> Android client patched from <a href='https://github.com/zerotier/ZeroTierOne' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>official client</a>. ZeroTier enables devices such as PCs, phones, servers and embedded devices to securely connect to peer-to-peer virtual networks. It combines the capabilities of VPN and SD-WAN, simplifying network management.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Self-hosted Moon Support</li><li>Add custom planet config via file and URL</li><li>View peers list</li></ul>
