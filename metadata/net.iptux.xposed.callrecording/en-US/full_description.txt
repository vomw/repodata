<p>As its name suggests, <i>Advanced Call Recording Settings for CyanogenMod / LineageOS</i> gives you advanced settings for the Call Recording feature that ships with CM/LOS.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>enable call recording for CyanogenMod / LineageOS</li><li>use Voice Call audio source for recording</li><li>record incoming calls or outgoing calls only</li><li>include contact name in recording file name (Android 9 / LOS 16.0 and before)</li><li>support CM 11.0, 12.0, 12.1, 13.0, LOS 14.0, 14.1, 15.1, 16.0, 17.1, 18.1, 19.1</li></ul><p>recording files are saved in:</p><ul><li><code>/sdcard/CallRecordings</code> (Android 9 / LOS 16.0 and before)</li><li><code>/sdcard/Music/Call Recordings</code> (Android 10 / LOS 17.1 and Android 11 / LOS 18.1)</li><li><code>/sdcard/Recordings/Call</code> recordings (Android 12 / LOS 19.1 and later)</li></ul><p><b>Note:</b> extra setup required for LSPosed, just enable this module and check the checkbox of Phone (<code>com.android.dialer</code>).</p>
