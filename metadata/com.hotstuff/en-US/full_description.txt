<p><i>Hot Stuff</i> is an offline app designed to help you quickly and accurately document belongings for proof of loss forms in personal property insurance claims.</p><p>With <i>Hot Stuff</i> you can:</p><ul><li>Create and store item information</li><li>Take and store related photos</li><li>Search through created records for specific items</li><li>See a summary of all created items</li><li>Export stored data to spreadsheet software like Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel</li><li>Read educational resources, such as a glossary of common insurance terminology, walkthroughs of app functionalities, and opinion-based articles</li><li>Customize the app to your preference of theme and currency</li></ul><p><i>Hot Stuff</i> will never cost money, run ads, or collect data without your consent (all information is kept on your device and can be exported to CSV, meaning your data always stays in your control).</p><p><i>Hot Stuff</i> is open-source software maintained by a community of volunteers, built on the idea that protecting your future should be free, private, and available to all. The source code is viewable on GitHub, and contributions are always welcome!</p>
