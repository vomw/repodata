<p><i>Microphone</i> is a simple app that outputs the sound of the built-in microphone.</p><p>To avoid acoustic feedback, the app works only when an audio cable is plugged in or a bluetooth audio receiver is connected to the smartphone.</p><p>The app is ad-free and has no additional settings, so it is easy to use.</p>
