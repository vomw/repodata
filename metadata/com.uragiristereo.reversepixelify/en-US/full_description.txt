<p><i>Reverse Pixelify</i> lets you revert/disable internal spoofing caused by custom ROMs. Some people don't like it when their device recognized as another device in apps.</p><p>Requirements: <a href="https://github.com/LSPosed/LSPosed" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">LSPosed</a>, supported ROM (see list in the Readme of the source repo).</p>
