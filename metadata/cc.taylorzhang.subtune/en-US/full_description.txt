<p><i>SubTune</i> is a free and open source music streaming Android client for Subsonic API (version 1.13.0 or higher) compatible servers (Subsonic, Navidrome, Airsonic, and more).</p><p><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Material 3 design</li><li>Browse albums, playlists</li><li>Search albums and songs</li><li>Max bitrate settings</li><li>Dark theme</li><li>Dynamic color</li><li>Lyrics</li></ul>
