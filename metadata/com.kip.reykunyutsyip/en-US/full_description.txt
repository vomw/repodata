<p><i>Reykunyutsyip</i> is an unofficial Android client app for the <a href="https://reykunyu.lu/" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Reykunyu Na'vi dictionary</a>, with Material design. The dictionary website itself is <a href="https://github.com/Willem3141/navi-reykunyu" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">also open-source</a>.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Automatically cached dictionary for searching even when you're offline</li><li>Offline translate mode with limited functionality</li><li>Online translate mode!</li><li>Supports searching in German, Estonian, English, Na'vi, French, Hungarian, Dutch, Polish, Russian and Swedish.</li><li>A really nice-looking Na'vi word card containing a bunch of information<ul><li>Here are some notable features</li><li>playable audio pronunciation examples</li><li>Rich text with Na'vi reference hyperlinks</li><li>Animal images :)</li></ul></li></ul><p><b>Reykunyu</b> ("someone who lets you discover things") is a dictionary in which you can search for Na'vi words and see the English translation and other information about that word. Reykunyu tries to give more detailed information than other dictionaries. The <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Na%CA%BCvi_language' target='_blank'>Naʼvi language</a> is a fictional constructed language originally made for the 2009 film Avatar. In the film franchise, the language is spoken by the Naʼvi, a race of sapient humanoids indigenous to the extraterrestrial moon Pandora.</p>
