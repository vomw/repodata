<p><i>Styx</i> aims to be a secure, open source web browser for Android devices. Its featue list includes…</p><ul><li>Sessions.</li><li>Tabs manager.</li><li>Bookmarks.</li><li>History.</li><li>Google search suggestions.</li><li>Keyboard support.</li><li>Webpage theming.</li><li>Desktop rendering.</li><li>Text To Speech (TTS)</li><li>Print mode.</li><li>Page-Tools.</li><li>Page source text editor.</li><li>3rd party app forwarding.</li><li>Android desktop support (Samsung Dex, EMUI Desktop).</li><li>Multiple search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, StartPage, DuckDuckGo, Mojeek, Searx etc.).</li><li>Incognito mode.</li><li>Orbot Proxy support and I2P support.</li><li>Ad-blocker.</li><li>Malware blocker.</li><li>Reader mode.</li><li>Various rendering mode: inverted, high contrast, grayscale.</li><li>Specific look & feel settings for portrait and landscape.</li><li>Optional pull-to-refresh.</li><li>Hardware accelerated rendering.</li><li>Rich tabs management settings.</li><li>Tabs recovery.</li><li>Full screen arrangeable tabs panel.</li><li>Basic bookmarks panel, import and export.</li><li>and much more...</li></ul>
