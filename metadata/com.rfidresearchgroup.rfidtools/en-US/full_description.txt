<p><i>RFID Tools</i> is a necessary research tool in the field of RFID. It includes proxmark3 universal client. The client comes from the latest RRG Iceman repo. You can use it to connect your Proxmark 3 RDV4.0 via Bluetooth or USB-OTG. Later, it will be compatible with more devices in the future.</p><p>For a list of compatible devices and other additional details, please visit the project site.</p>
