<p><i>Dev Tiles</i> provides Android Quick Settings tiles for developers. Works on Android 7.0+.</p><p>After installing the app, you need to grant it two permissions:</p><ul><li>adb shell pm grant xyz.mustafaali.devqstiles android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS</li><li>adb shell pm grant xyz.mustafaali.devqstiles android.permission.DUMP</li></ul><p>After that, you can use the provided tiles to toggle USB Debugging, keep screen on <i>when connected to a computer</i>, and more.</p>
