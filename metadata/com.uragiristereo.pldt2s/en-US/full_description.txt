<p>This is an Xposed/LSPosed module to enable double tap to sleep functionality on Pixel Launcher. It was tested on Pixel Launcher 12 (906) on Android 12.1.</p><p>Requirements: <a href='https://github.com/LSPosed/LSPosed' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>LSPosed</a> & [Magisk](https://github.com/topjohnwu/Magisk).</p><p><b>Note:</b> You need to grant superuser access to Pixel Launcher in order to work.</p>
