<p><i>PodClassic</i> simulates the iPod Classic. The app is localized for Chinese and English, and intuitive to use:</p><ul><li><b>Wheel:</b> Slide the red part to move the cursor up and down</li><li><b>Button:</b> Tap the black middle button to confirm</li><li><b>Button:</b> Press and hold the middle button to collect songs/folders containing songs/albums/artists and play them in the collection</li><li><b>Wheel icons:</b> The four icons function as shown</li></ul><p><b>NOTE:</b> the app's repo and Github account have been deleted, so this app will no longer receive updates.</p>
