<p><i>Readify</i> is a light, modern, free and opensource project which keeps you inform by fetching your websites/blogs and displaying them in a mobile-optimized way. It <i>does not</i> synchronize with any online feed reader.</p><p>Based on spaRSS which based on Flym and Sparse RSS, <i>Readify</i> checks RSS/Atom news feeds, polling for updates from the device on a regular basis. Fetched items are available for offline reading.</p>
