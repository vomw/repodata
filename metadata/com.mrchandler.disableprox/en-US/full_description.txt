<p>This uses the Xposed Framework in order to the disable and modify sensors on a device. It requires you to not only be rooted, but to also have Xposed installed. If you don’t know what either of those are, this app will not work on your phone.</p><p>This app can be used to disable your proximity sensor along with other sensors on your device.</p><p>The proximity sensor is a sensor in most phones that tells the phone when the phone is near another object (generally a face.) If your screen frequently goes black when on a phone call and you can’t use it until the other party hangs up, this should fix your problem.</p><p><br><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li>Can disable and modify most sensors on your phone (without requiring a restart).</li><li>Can use Tasker to control sensor statuses. (Pro Version)</li><li>Can use a blacklist/whitelist in order to decide which apps should use or ignore sensor settings you’ve set. (Pro Version)</li></ul><p>If you have already purchased the Tasker In-App Purchase, you have the Pro Version already (and therefore the new blacklist/whitelist support.)</p><p>If you have issues, check the <a href='https://forum.xda-developers.com/xposed/modules/mod-disable-proximity-t2798887/post53766106' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>support thread at XDA</a> or send me an email and we can work through your problem.</p>
