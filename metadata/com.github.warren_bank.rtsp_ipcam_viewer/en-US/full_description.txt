<p><i>RTSP IP-Cam Viewer</i> is an Android app to watch RTSP video streams; this format is typical of inexpensive IP security cams.</p><p><br><b>Goals:</b></p><ul><li>an extremely light-weight open-source app with minimal features and minimal UI</li><li>ability to add video streams (including via import of <a href='https://github.com/warren-bank/Android-RTSP-IPCam-Viewer/blob/master/.etc/sample_file_import_data/video_streams.json' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>JSON text files</a>)</li><li>display of enabled video streams in list- and grid-view, full-screen view of a single high-res video stream</li></ul><p><b>Notes:</b></p><ul><li>this app does not require the latest Android versions, so your old device can be put to a new purpose with this</li><li>when videos are displayed in list/grid views: audio is mute; click to open video in full-screen view; long click to toggle pause/play</li><li>when a video is displayed in full-screen view: audio is not mute; controls are visible</li></ul>
