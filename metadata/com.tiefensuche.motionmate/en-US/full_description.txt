<p><i>MotionMate</i> is a very simple and lightweight step counter app for Android that tracks your daily steps and displays the results by selecting a specific day from the calendar view and presents the values in a nice weekly bar chart as well as some other statistics.</p><p>It uses the built-in step counter sensor, if it is present on the device and uses a fallback implementation based on the accelerometer sensor otherwise.</p><p>It requires no internet access and no special permissions.</p>
