<p><i>Roc for Android</i> is an app implementing Roc sender and receiver. Roc features real-time streaming with guaranteed latency, restoring lost packets using Forward Erasure Correction codes, CD-quality audio and much more. Compatible senders and receivers include:</p><ul><li><a href='https://roc-streaming.org/toolkit/docs/running/pulseaudio_modules.html' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>PulseAudio modules</a></li><li><a href='https://roc-streaming.org/toolkit/docs/running/command_line_tools.html' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>command-line tools</a> available for multiple operating systems</li></ul><p><b>Features:</b></p><ul><li><b>receive</b> sound from remote Roc-compatible sender and play to local audio device</li><li><b>record</b> sound from local audio device and send to remote Roc-compatible receiver</li></ul>
