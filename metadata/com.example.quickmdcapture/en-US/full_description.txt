<p><i>QuickMDCapture</i> allows you to quickly create notes in Markdown format from the notification bar. This allows you to quickly create notes, as the notification panel can be opened quickly without distracting you from another app, such as the e-book you are reading. Originally, the app was created to work with <a href='https://obsidian.md/' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>Obsidian</a>, but it can also be used with other note-taking apps that support the .md format if needed.</p><p>The app creates a persistent notification in the notification panel.</p>
