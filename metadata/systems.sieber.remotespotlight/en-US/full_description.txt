<p>With this app you can control your computer’s keyboard and mouse (using a touchpad). Furthermore, you can project a digital laser pointer dot on your screen or projector, which is controlled by the movement of your Android device.</p><p>Advantages:</p><ul><li>control your computer from your couch</li><li>when your screen output is being recorded on presentations, the laser pointer can also be recorded</li><li>the digital laser pointer point is better visible in bright rooms</li><li>you can simultaneously flip the slides back and forth on your Android device and control the mouse</li><li>you can use the app as barcode/QR code scanner for your computer</li></ul><p>Please download the free software for your PC (Linux, macOS and Windows) <a href='https://sieber.systems/s/rp' target='blank' rel='nofollow'>from here</a>. You can find <a href='https://github.com/schorschii/RemotePointer-Server' target='_blank' rel='nofollow'>its source at Github</a>.</p>
